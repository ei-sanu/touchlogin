LPU Wi-Fi Auto Login Script

This script automatically logs you into the LPU 24 Online Internet Portal every time you connect to Wi-Fi.
No more typing your registration number, entering your password, or ticking the Terms & Conditions checkbox — the script fills everything and logs you in instantly.

🚀 Features

Auto-fills your LPU registration number

Auto-fills your Wi-Fi password

Automatically accepts Terms & Conditions

Bypasses disabled login button

Triggers LPU’s internal validation function (if available)

Automatically clicks the login button

Works immediately on page load

🔧 How to Use

Open the LPU Wi-Fi login page in your browser.

Open the browser’s Developer Console (F12 → Console tab).

Paste the entire script into the console.

Replace:

"YOUR LPU REG NUMBER" → with your own Reg. No.

"YOUR WIFI PASSWORD" → with your actual password

Press Enter.

The script will handle the entire login process automatically.
